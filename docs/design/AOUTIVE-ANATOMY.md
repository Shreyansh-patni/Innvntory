# AOUTIVE ANATOMY

**Purpose:** Document the recurring visual language of the Aoutive site to serve as the primary visual reference for Innvntory.

## Global Reusable Patterns

### 1. The Canvas
- **Observation:** The base page background is never pure white. It uses a very light off-white/cream canvas (`#f7f7f7` or similar). 
- **Pattern:** Sections are separated by soft padding rather than hard borders where possible, letting the canvas breathe.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Innvntory will use a warm or cool off-white canvas for the application background, reserving pure white (`#ffffff`) for elevated cards and content areas.

### 2. Header & Navigation
- **Observation:** The header is typically contained, floating, or clearly demarcated with minimal height, using a simple logo on the left and a CTA on the right.
- **Pattern:** Links are understated with subtle hover states.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** The marketing site will use a similar floating/clean header. The SaaS app will use a dense top-nav or sidebar but retain the visual cleanliness (minimal borders, muted text for inactive links).

### 3. Typography & Editorial Feel
- **Observation:** Large, clean typography with tight tracking (letter-spacing) for display headers, and readable, generously spaced body copy.
- **Pattern:** Fonts include `Geist`, `Space Grotesk`, and `Instrument Sans`.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Adopt a similar premium sans-serif stack (e.g., Geist or Inter) that looks sharp and technical but remains highly readable for data-dense tables.

### 4. Cards & Surfaces
- **Observation:** Cards use very subtle border radii (e.g., 8px, 12px, 16px) and extremely faint hairlines (1px borders) rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Pattern:** Clean white surface (`#ffffff`) on the off-white canvas.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Adopt hairline-only depth for dashboard metrics, inventory cards, and tables. Avoid aggressive shadows.

### 5. Buttons & Controls
- **Observation:** Buttons use pill-like or moderately rounded shapes (8px, 10px, 100px). Primary actions use a strong, restrained accent color; secondary actions are often outlines or subtle surface fills.
- **Pattern:** Compact height, sharp text.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Standardize on 8px radius for SaaS inputs/buttons for a more technical, "developer-tools" feel (influenced by Cursor), reserving pill shapes for badges or marketing CTAs.

## Feature-Level Patterns

### Hero Section
- **Observation:** Generous whitespace, massive display typography (up to 64px+), centered alignment, leading into a strong product visual.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Use this for the marketing homepage. The app dashboard will skip the hero and jump straight to actionable metrics.

### Article / Feature Cards
- **Observation:** Image on top (or structured icon layout), clean typography below, minimal borders.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Use for empty states, onboarding guides, or integration discovery in the app.

### Data & Metrics
- **Observation:** Numbers are presented with strong hierarchy—large values, muted labels.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Dashboard metric cards will follow this exact pattern: large numeric value (e.g., 24px/32px), subtle secondary text for percentage changes, faint 1px border.

## One-Off Patterns
- **Marketing Illustrations:** Aoutive uses specific abstract assets.
- **Innvntory Adaptation:** Do not copy Aoutive's abstract assets. Innvntory will use product screenshots or UI abstractions as its artwork.
